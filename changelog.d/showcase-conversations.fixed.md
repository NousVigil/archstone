- **The Showcase's searches invented stays and ignored `budget` and `preferences`.** `wanderlust_search` returned made-up "Generated Stay" rows for any city but Lisbon, whose ids resolved nowhere, and the deprecated `tourism_search` hashed the destination and ignored `budget`. Both now read one catalogue (Lisbon, Porto, Barcelona, Nice, Bucharest) through one destination resolver that tolerates case, diacritics, aliases (`Lisboa`, `Bucuresti`) and `City, Country`; an unknown place is an honest empty result and every returned id resolves on details, photos, page, quote and availability. `budget` is a per-night EUR ceiling on both searches (another currency, or dates that are not an ISO range, are a 400). Both declare `preferences` (`pets`, `breakfast`, `family`; synonyms such as `pet-friendly`, `cat`, `dog` fold in, other tags are ignored), and `wanderlust.Stay` and `wanderlust.StayDetails` declare `petPolicy`. Adds a deterministic conversation suite and `pnpm showcase:conversations`, a local script that drives a live endpoint with a real model.
